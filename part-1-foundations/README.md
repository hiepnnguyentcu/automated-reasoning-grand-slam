# Part 1: Foundations

## Objective

Build the logic, computability and semantics needed to read and prove results in automated reasoning without assistance.

This part is concepts only, and learning is checked with closed-book proofs and exercises.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Objective | Proof of mastery |
|--:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Mathematical Logic](01-mathematical-logic/) | Reason precisely about propositional and first-order logic and prove their central metatheorems. | Prove the completeness of a propositional proof system by hand in a closed-book exam. |
| 2 | [Decidability and Complexity](02-decidability-and-complexity/) | Determine how hard a reasoning problem is and what that implies for the choice of algorithm. | Classify unseen reasoning problems by decidability and complexity and justify each answer with a reduction. |
| 3 | [Semantics and Types](03-semantics-and-types/) | Give programming languages precise meaning so that claims about programs can be stated and proved. | Prove type soundness for a small typed language by hand in a closed-book exam. |
