# Chapter 20: New Reasoning Methods

Part 7: Original Research.

## Objective

Contribute new decision procedures, heuristics or search strategies and show that they work at scale.

## Proof of mastery

Publish a first-author paper at a leading venue and enter a public solver competition.

## Topics

| Topic | Objective |
|---|---|
| [Finding Problems](finding-problems/) | Choose an open problem that is important, tractable and suited to your strengths. |
| [New Decision Procedures](new-decision-procedures/) | Design a decision procedure or theory extension and prove it sound and complete where possible. |
| [Search Strategies](search-strategies/) | Propose and test a new heuristic or search strategy on a real class of problems. |
| [Parallel and Cloud Solving](parallel-and-cloud-solving/) | Explain and experiment with portfolio, cube-and-conquer and distributed solving at scale. |
| [Trust and Proof Production](trust-and-proof-production/) | Make results independently checkable through proofs and certified checkers. |
| [Experimental Evaluation](experimental-evaluation/) | Design experiments that would survive review, including sound benchmarks and statistics. |
| [Writing and Publishing](writing-and-publishing/) | Write, submit and revise papers for venues such as CAV and FMCAD. |
| [Competitions and Community](competitions-and-community/) | Enter a solver competition and build professional relationships through talks and questions. |
